The output does not meet standards.

The goal was that there would be two versions of each approach:

1. Photorealistic
2. Stylized

Neither of the versions shown on any evidence backed renderer meets the goal.

On photorealistic I expected high-quality, photorealistic, with depth of field blurring, golden hour or similar lighting situation, diorama setup, and higher fidelity voxel model (not just a low-poly approximation). A door that is made up of only 10 voxel cubes is too low fidelity, and I expected better. 

I believe our lack of shaders and model sources is a big cause of this. But you can also do better finding and building voxel models. I believe in you, I believe you can do better.

See the reference photos at: research/experiments/voxel-rendering/docs/refine-it/references

I believe we are missing materials and shaders to make the output meet the goal. Lighting and glass/water and depth of field and cenematic lighting and shadows are all important to make the output look realistic.

Work on improving the shaders, textures, lighting, and model sources to meet the goal.
