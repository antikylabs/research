## Sponza model and PBR textures

The bundled scene descends from the Sponza model by Marko Dabrovic, improved by
Frank Meinl at Crytek, with modifications by Morgan McGuire and PBR textures by
Alexandre Pestana. Preserve the complete upstream usage and attribution history
published by the Khronos glTF Sample Models project:

https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Models/blob/main/2.0/Sponza/README.md

The source demo's renderer, shaders, unused lens-dirt image, and duplicate
`Sponza.bin.gz` are not included in this benchmark data directory.
